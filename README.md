# superstore-project
Superstore Business Intelligence: Executive Dashboard for AnalystLab Africa Week 2.

Key Business Questions Addressed

1. What is the overall sales performance and profitability baseline?
2. Which geographic regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
4. Which product categories and sub-categories perform best vs. worst?
5. What seasonal trends exist over time?
6. What strategic recommendations should management implement to improve business performance?

---

 Dashboard Key Features & Visualisations
* **KPI Header Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Sales, and Profit Margin.
* **Sales Trend Analysis:** Line chart showing monthly/quarterly sales performance over time.
* **Geographic Performance:** Interactive map highlighting sales and profitability by state.
* **Category & Sub-Category Analysis:** Bar/Column charts pinpointing top profit drivers (Technology) and margin-bleeding categories (Furniture/Tables).
* **Interactive Cross-Filtering:** Enabled action filters allowing dynamic drill-down by region, category, and segment.

---

 Key Business Insights
* **Revenue Baseline:** Total revenue reached **~$2.3M** with **~$286.4K** in net profit (12.5% profit margin) across **5,009 orders**.
* **Regional Dominance:** The **West** and **East** regions drive over **60%** of total revenue.
* **Segment Contribution:** The **Consumer** segment is the largest revenue contributor (~50%), followed by Corporate (~30%) and Home Office (~20%).
* **Margin Bleed:** Heavy discounting on **Tables** and **Bookcases** generates net losses despite high sales volume.

---

 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Cap Discount Rates:** Implement a strict **15% maximum discount cap** on loss-making items (*Tables* & *Bookcases*).
2. **Expand B2B Accounts:** Launch a Corporate & Home Office volume-incentive program to boost off-peak revenue and Average Order Value (AOV).
3. **Q4 Peak Inventory Readiness:** Optimize stock for top-margin items (*Copiers*, *Phones*) ahead of the seasonal Q4 sales surge.
4. **Product Bundling:** Package low-margin Furniture items into office hardware bundles alongside high-margin Technology products.
